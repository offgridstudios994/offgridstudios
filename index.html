How to start

Three steps from curious to playing.

No audition, no prior experience. Just one lesson to see if it fits — the rest follows your pace.

01

Book a trial

Pick a time that suits you. One 45-minute lesson on the instrument you're curious about — no commitment, no fee to enrol.

02

Meet your teacher

We match you to a tutor by instrument, level and language — Arabic, English or French — so the first lesson already feels like the right one.

03

Find your rhythm

Weekly lessons, termly recitals, and a place in an ensemble when you're ready to play alongside others.
