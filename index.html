How to start

Three steps from curious to your first set.

No audition, no prior experience. Just one lesson to see if it fits — the rest follows your pace.

01

Book a trial

Pick a time that suits you. One 45-minute lesson on the deck or DAW you're curious about — no commitment, no fee to enrol.

02

Meet your teacher

We match you to a tutor by style, level and gear — turntablism, controllers, production — so the first lesson already feels like the right one.

03

Find your rhythm

Weekly lessons, termly showcases, and a back-to-back slot in the crew when you're ready to play alongside others.
